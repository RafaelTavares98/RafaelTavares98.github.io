About Me

A short summary of my career so far.

I am Rafael Tavares, 27 years old, based in Goiânia, Brazil. I am looking for a remote role with an international team.

I spent three years in logistics and operations at Coty. I started as an Expedition Assistant, moved to Operational Production Analyst, and then to Freight Audit and Payment. Freight audit built my technical foundation. My core stack is SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, and advanced Excel. I use it to automate workflows and to analyze operational data.

My current title is Analytics and AI Consultant. The core of the work is data analysis, business intelligence, and process automation. On top of that I build AI systems: MCP servers, LLM applications, and internal tools with Python, FastAPI, and Docker. I also run security monitoring with Wazuh and triage vulnerabilities across a Linux server fleet.

From July 2025 to February 2026 I was a volunteer machine learning researcher with the AKCIT group at the Federal University of Goiás (UFG). There I studied MLOps and implemented reinforcement learning models with DQN and PPO.

I hold a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt and the DataCamp Associate Data Engineer certificate. My long-term goal is data engineering or machine learning engineering. Outside work I train with weights, cycle, and run.
