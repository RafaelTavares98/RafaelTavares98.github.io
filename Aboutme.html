About me?

I am Rafael Tavares, 27, based in Goiânia, Brazil. I am currently looking to transition into the international remote market.

I have three years of hands-on experience in logistics and operations at Coty. I started as an Operational Production Analyst and later moved to Freight Audit. That transition is where I built my technical foundation. My core stack is SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, and Advanced Excel. I use these tools to automate workflows and analyze data directly from the operation.

To expand my technical limits, I joined a research group at the Federal University of Goiás (UFG) to study MLOps and implement Reinforcement Learning models.

My long-term goal is to become a Data Engineer or Machine Learning Engineer.

When I am not coding or analyzing data, my main hobbies are training Jiu-Jitsu and horseback riding.
