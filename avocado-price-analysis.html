Avocado Price Analysis

Does price move volume? Which regions and varieties lead U.S. avocado sales? Answered in PostgreSQL.

Case description

Weekly Hass avocado sales data, 2015 to 2018: average price, total volume, and volume by PLU code (small, large, extra-large), across U.S. regions. Loaded into PostgreSQL and queried directly, no notebook layer.

Findings

  • Price and volume correlate at -0.34. Price moves volume, but weakly, other factors matter more.
  • West, California, and SouthCentral are the highest-volume regions in the dataset.
  • Large Hass (PLU4225) narrowly outsells small Hass (PLU4046), 5.39B to 5.35B units. Extra-large (PLU4770) is a distant third.
  • Los Angeles holds a steady ~8-9% share of total U.S. volume across full years.
