Credit Card Approval

Binary classification model that predicts the odds of credit card approval before a hard inquiry hits the applicant's credit score.

Business problem

Every hard credit inquiry lowers an applicant's credit score, whether or not the card gets approved. This model estimates the probability of approval beforehand, so an applicant can decide whether to apply without the score hit.

Dataset: Kaggle, credit card approval prediction. Stack: Python, scikit-learn, Streamlit for the interface.

Status

This page is a study reference, not finished original work. It walks through an existing binary classification project to learn the approach before rebuilding it with different data and framing.
