Data job postings analysis with Power BI

An interactive Power BI exploration of the global data job market, covering top paying salaries, skills, and optimal skills demand across roles.

Case Description

Based on the comprehensive dataset created by data analyst Luke Barousse, this project explores the modern data ecosystem. Sourced from online listings, it covers over 785,000 job postings from companies across multiple countries, tracking real-world employer demands, technical skill requirements, and salary trends.

To uncover actionable career insights, the analysis was structured around 3 main objectives:

  • Identify the top data jobs based on market demand and average salaries.
  • Determine the most demanded skills across these technical roles.
  • Highlight "optimal skills" that offer the best balance of high demand and high pay.

Data Insights

1. Top Data Jobs

The job market is heavily concentrated in core technical roles. Data Analysts and Data Engineers dominate the landscape, representing the vast majority of the postings. Data Analysts lead in sheer volume (over 28% of postings), while Data Engineers follow closely (over 23%) but command a higher average baseline salary ($10.3K vs $7.1K). This highlights a clear path: strong demand for foundational analysis, with significant financial incentives for those moving into data architecture and engineering.

2. Top Data Skills

When evaluating technical requirements, two languages stand completely apart from the rest: SQL (58.3%) and Python (57.7%) are the undisputed pillars of the data profession. Beyond these core programming languages, employers heavily demand Cloud technologies (like AWS and Redshift) and Visualization/BI tools (such as Power BI and Looker). Mastering a combination of a querying language, a scripting language, and a visualization tool is the baseline for entering the field.

3. Optimal Skills (High Demand & High Pay)

By plotting skill demand against average pay, we can identify the most strategic areas for professional development. Skills located in the upper-right quadrant of our analysis—such as Python, SQL, AWS, and Redshift—represent the "sweet spot". These technologies not only have an extremely high market adoption rate but are also tied to the most lucrative salary brackets in the industry, making them the most optimal skills to learn.
